Application: YouTube Shorts Autopilot Desktop FitIn60s

Effective Date: October 25, 2026
Last Updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YouTube Shorts Autopilot Desktop FitIn60s ("Application") handles information when its authorized user uses the application to perform supported YouTube operations.

1. About the Application

The Application is a desktop-based tool intended to help its authorized user upload and schedule video content on YouTube.

The Application uses Google OAuth and YouTube API Services to perform operations authorized by the user.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, the Application may access:

  • Google OAuth authorization tokens.
  • YouTube channel information needed for authorized operations.
  • Video files selected for uploading.
  • Video titles, descriptions, tags, and publication settings.
  • Upload status and related API responses.

The Application accesses information necessary for its functionality and within the permissions granted by the user.

3. Google Drive and Video Files

Video files may be stored in Google Drive before being uploaded to YouTube. The Application's access to Google Drive depends on whether it requests and receives the relevant Google Drive permissions.

The Application processes user-selected video files for the requested upload operation. Its actual access to Drive files depends on its implementation and permissions.

4. How Information Is Used

The Application uses authorized information to:

  • Authorize access to the user's YouTube channel.
  • Upload selected videos.
  • Submit video metadata and publication settings.
  • Schedule publication where supported.
  • Monitor upload status and troubleshoot errors.

YouTube API data is handled in accordance with the applicable YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

5. Storage and Security

The Application runs on the user's computer. Video files may be stored in Google Drive. OAuth tokens and other application data may be stored locally or elsewhere, depending on the actual implementation.

Before publication, the developer must ensure that this description accurately identifies where OAuth tokens and other sensitive information are stored and how they are protected.

Users should protect their computer, Google account, application files, and any credentials or tokens.

6. Sharing of Information

Information required for authorized YouTube operations is transmitted to Google services through the relevant APIs.

The Application does not sell personal information. Any additional third-party processing, if applicable, must be accurately disclosed here before publication.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The Application should retain information only as long as necessary for its intended functionality and applicable policy requirements.

Local files, cached data, and OAuth tokens may be removed through the relevant deletion procedure supported by the installed application or its local configuration.

Deleting a local file does not necessarily delete an uploaded YouTube video or a source video stored in Google Drive. Those items may need to be deleted separately through the relevant service.

For data deletion requests, contact the developer using the email address listed below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law and YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
